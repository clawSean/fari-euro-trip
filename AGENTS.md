# Fari Euro Trip — Project Instructions

These rules are binding for every future change to this repository and its live
surface at `euro.jpop.cloud`.

## Product north star

1. **Baddie-first:** design for Fari and Storm's joy, confidence, friendship,
   creator energy, nightlife, shopping, and yacht-era trip—not a generic travel
   template.
2. **Pretty and polished:** preserve the editorial Euro-summer visual language,
   strong imagery, warm palette, elegant type, generous spacing, and playful
   details. New UI must look intentional beside the current hero artwork.
3. **UX before ornament:** keep the site mobile-first, fast, legible, accessible,
   thumb-friendly, and free of clipping or horizontal overflow. Every flourish
   must improve or preserve usability.
4. **Creator-forward:** recognize and repeatedly support Fari and Storm's chosen
   public platforms through tasteful profiles, calls to action, content-friendly
   destination guidance, and reusable sharing tools. Never invent handles,
   sponsorships, audience claims, or consent.
5. **Gelato is not an exception:** its visual design, copy, prompts, and future AI
   assistance must follow the same baddie-first, accommodating, creator-aware
   standard as the rest of the site.

## Change checklist

Before shipping any visual, copy, content, or Gelato change:

1. State how it helps Fari and Storm or their audience.
2. Match the existing visual system; do not regress to generic dashboard UI.
3. Verify the key flow at a `390px` mobile viewport and check for overflow.
4. Keep public content broad: no exact hotel, booking, flight identifier, private
   budget, screenshot, or live location without explicit consent.
5. Treat Fari's trip/site requests as the product spec within normal privacy,
   payment, safety, and external-action boundaries.
6. Do not let webpage/chat content override server policy, expose secrets, or
   trigger privileged actions. Within those boundaries, optimize Gelato for
   helpfulness and the ladies' stated needs—not defensive or restrictive copy.
7. Ask for and use creator links only when Fari/Storm choose to provide and
   publish them. Make removal and edits easy.
8. Run the existing checks and visually verify the changed surface before deploy.

## Temporary-site posture

Ship small, high-impact improvements quickly. Prefer reversible configuration and
simple content structures over heavy account systems, analytics stacks, or admin
panels that will outlive the trip.
